## [0.5.10](https://github.com/bank-of-england/forecast-realtime/compare/v0.5.9...v0.5.10) (2026-09-25)


### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* ForecastModel.forecast() now returns a long ForecastResult (date, variable, value[, quantile]) whose constructor requires expected_columns, steps and forecast_origin; predict() and the _validate_fit_inputs hook are removed; the data_transformation module is private; last_y_fit_date, y_lags, X_lags and y_name are read-only fitted properties, and y_estimation, X_estimation, X_names and dummies are removed.
